### 1. COLOR SYSTEM

#### Strategic Rationale

The color palette is built around **purple** as the primary brand color (communicating intelligence, creativity, and premium positioning) with **pink** as an accent for highlights and CTAs. The palette is deliberately restrained — a premium agency aesthetic that avoids both the cold sterility of pure blue-based tech brands and the overly warm tones of traditional agencies.

#### Primary Colors

| Color | HEX | RGB | HSL | Usage |
|-------|-----|-----|-----|-------|
| Primary Purple | `#6B5FA9` | rgb(107, 95, 169) | hsl(249, 30%, 52%) | Brand, CTAs, links |
| Light Purple | `#8D81E3` | rgb(141, 129, 227) | hsl(244, 39%, 70%) | Hover states, accents |
| Dark Purple | `#4B4275` | rgb(75, 66, 117) | hsl(249, 22%, 36%) | Dark mode, emphasis |

### PAGE-INDEPENDENT ARCHITECTURE

Burify Labs follows a **strict page-independent architecture**. Every page must be capable of existing, styling, and functioning independently from other pages.

---

### ARCHITECTURE PRINCIPLES

#### 1. Global Foundation (Genuine Globals Only)

The global CSS layer contains ONLY:
- CSS reset
- Design tokens (CSS variables)
- Typography defaults
- Accessibility defaults
- Universal utility classes
